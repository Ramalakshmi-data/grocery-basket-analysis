Category Correlation Matrix

Which categories get bought together, customer by customer

How to read this

For each customer we added up their total spend (quantity × unit price) in every category. Then, for each pair of categories, we asked: do customers who spend more on one also spend more on the other? That is a Pearson correlation, from −1 (one goes up as the other goes down) through 0 (no link) to +1 (they rise and fall together).

Categories run in the store's own order (Milk → Chocolates), not alphabetically. Green cells are bought together; clay cells trade off.
